# A5: scoped finalized fee counters

Standard v3 isolated implementation. No existing program, funded launch or hosted deployment was changed.

- PostgreSQL migration 29 stores finalized fee-state counters by genesis, issuer and campaign. Ordered slots, immutable same-slot fingerprints, lease fencing, cursor CAS and atomic successor scheduling protect publication.
- `fee-index` is a read job in the indexing lane, separate from financial harvest/economics. Counters do not depend on retained swap history. The old v2 worker still leases only its existing market job class.
- Campaign and canonical fee state are read together at finalized commitment. Checks cover ownership, campaign binding, Standard policy, cumulative entitlements and conservation. Missing setup and invalid state remain explicit. Rounding dust stays in custody.
- Browser reads use the authenticated database reader, bounded cache and single-flight; no per-viewer RPC. Provider block time and observation age determine staleness. Collected coin tokens, completed burns and pending burns are distinct. Exact small amounts remain accessible.
- A compact panel shares the chart polling session. Details expose treasury/dev paid versus accrued, pending SOL, dust, finalized slot and fee-state address. Pool rate comes from the sealed terms.
