# Build 6: parent claims close at the upgrade and the reserves burn, parent budgets buy and burn the coin

Decided by the owner on 24 September 2026. Source patched the same day. Not built by CI, not deployed.

**Summary.** Free parent rewards close the moment build 6 is live (window constant zero, owner decision 24 Sep 2026); anyone can then burn what was not claimed, which is the whole 100M reserve today.
The two parent buyback budgets stop buying Fartcoin and Buttcoin and buy and burn the child coin instead, through the
campaign's own pool. Every live account keeps its bytes; the owner runs one program upgrade and nothing else.

## What changes for holders

- **Parent claims close.** With the window at zero seconds, no parent claim is possible once build 6 is live.
  After that the claim button must say the window is closed; the program refuses the claim with error 45.
- **Shartcoin window.** Launch time on chain: `1790213669` (2026-09-24 01:34:29 UTC). Window closes at
  `1790213669`: the launch time itself, because PARENT_CLAIM_WINDOW is zero.
- **Unclaimed rewards are burned.** After the window anyone can send tag 11 once. It burns exactly the unclaimed part of
  each 5 % reserve from launch custody. Participant claims, dev claims and refunds are untouched and have no window.
- **Parent buybacks stop.** The budgets already reserved for the parents and every future 25/168 + 25/168 share buy the
  child coin on its own pool and burn it. Live budgets at the time of writing: about 16.2 SOL on the Fartcoin side and
  79.5 SOL on the Buttcoin side. Nothing else about fees changes: treasury 98/168 and dev 20/168 stay.

## Where the launch time comes from

- **Campaign account, offset 232**, written by tag 6 (`launch_time`, 8 bytes, unix seconds). Verified on the live
  campaign `9FjwHicbkzP17NEW94UasBa3LfWtqmsmddzstq8UqKxP`: `1790213669`, phase 3, no distribution program.
- **No one-time recording call is needed.** The program derives `expires_at = launch_time + PARENT_CLAIM_WINDOW` (zero) on every call.
  The owner's fallback design (a creator-signed instruction writing the expiry into the parents account) is not built.

## Instruction changes

### Tag 10, parent claim (changed)

- **New rule.** Refused with error 45 when the clock is at or past `launch_time + PARENT_CLAIM_WINDOW` (zero, so every claim from the upgrade on). A claim at
  `expires_at - 1` still goes through; a replay of an already paid claim after the window is also refused with 45.
- **Accounts and body.** Unchanged (11 accounts, 18-byte body plus proof).

### Tag 11, burn expired parent reserves (new)

- **Who.** Anyone. No signer, no body. Refused with error 46 while the window is open.
- **What it burns.** For each parent: `reserve - claimed - burned`, where reserve is 5 % of the original supply
  (`50,000,000,000,000` raw units), claimed is what tag 10 paid, burned is what an earlier tag 11 recorded. Both
  remainders go in one Token burn from launch custody signed by the launch authority. A second call burns nothing.
- **Accounts.**
  0. campaign (read)
  1. parents account, PDA `["parents", campaign]` (writable)
  2. launch authority, PDA `["launch_authority", campaign]`
  3. child mint (writable)
  4. launch custody, the launch authority's associated token account for the child mint (writable)
  5. Token program
- **Written bytes in KIDSPAR1** (256 bytes, previously all zero from 224):
  - `224..232` burned for parent A (u64)
  - `232..240` burned for parent B (u64)
  - `240..248` unix time of the first tag 11 run (u64), even when nothing was left to burn
  - `248..256` still unused
- **Errors.** 46 window open, 40 distribution activated, 30 any account or balance mismatch, 10 corrupt counters.

### Tag 27, buy and burn the child coin (new)

- **Who.** The creator keeper, as for every fee instruction. Body 24 bytes: `amount` u64 lamports of WSOL,
  `min_out` u64 raw coin units, `expiry` i64 unix seconds.
- **Limits.** `amount` at most `500,000,000` lamports and at most pending A + pending B. `expiry` between now and
  now + 120 s. `min_out` at least the floor: the constant-product quote from the pool's live reserves after the 2.5 %
  fee, minus 1 %. The keeper may pass a higher `min_out`, never a lower one.
- **What happens.** WSOL custody pays `amount` into the pool, the coin custody receives at least `min_out`, every
  received unit is burned from custody, and the program checks WSOL custody fell by exactly `amount`, coin custody is
  back to where it was and the mint supply fell by the received amount.
- **Bookkeeping.** `spent_a` grows first until parent A's pending budget is used up, then `spent_b`;
  `burned_child` grows by the received amount. `parent_a`, `parent_b`, `burned_a`, `burned_b` and the totals do not
  move. The fee state layout is unchanged.
- **Accounts.**
  0. campaign
  1. creator keeper (signer)
  2. fee state, PDA `["fees", campaign]` (writable)
  3. fee authority, PDA `["fee_authority", campaign]`
  4. WSOL custody, fee authority's associated token account for WSOL (writable)
  5. coin custody, fee authority's associated token account for the child mint (writable)
  6. the campaign's pool, must equal campaign offset 240 (writable)
  7. AMM config of that pool (index 7, `ESLj2Rzmvn3RhDo4Z18hY1wYmGyC9xM4ZtRXhvoFkDAi` on mainnet)
  8. CPMM vault authority, PDA `["vault_and_lp_mint_auth_seed"]` under the CPMM program
  9. the pool's WSOL vault (writable)
  10. the pool's coin vault (writable)
  11. WSOL mint
  12. child mint (writable, the burn needs it)
  13. the pool's observation account (writable)
  14. CPMM program `CPMMoo8L3F4NbTegBCKVNunggL7H1ZpdTHKxQB5qKP1C`
  15. Token program
- **Errors.** 60 for every refused check, 61 for a counter that would not add up, 62 for a quote the fee consumes.

### Tags 22, 24 and 25 (retired)

- Refused with `InvalidInstructionData` before any account is read. Their code is removed from the program; the
  Jupiter constants and route parser went with it. A keeper of build 5 that still sends tag 25 gets this error and
  nothing moves.

### Everything else

- Tags 0 to 9, 20, 21, 23 and 26: unchanged behaviour and bytes. Campaign, receipts, KIDSPAR1, KIDSPCL1, fee state and
  claim PDAs keep their layouts. No live account is migrated.

## What the keeper needs (not in this patch)

- **Plan one buyback, not two.** When the live build lists `child-buyback`, `feePlan` should emit one
  `buy-burn-child` operation with `amount = pendingA + pendingB` instead of a `buy-burn` per parent, and never emit
  `buy-burn` (tag 24 or 25) on such a build.
- **Build tag 27.** Quote on the campaign's own pool with `boundedQuote(connection, WSOL, childMint, slice, state.pool)`;
  its `minOutput` is already 99 % of the spot quote, which equals the program floor. Slice at most 0.5 SOL and at most the
  combined pending budget. Expiry `chainTime + 90`. Account order as listed above, account 12 writable.
- **Keep the parent-turns logic out of it.** `lastBuybackParent` and the Jupiter route, price guard and slippage
  settings apply to tags 24 and 25 only.
- **Send tag 11 once after the window.** A small permissionless call; the keeper can send it on the first tick past
  `expires_at`, or the owner sends it by hand. It is safe to repeat.
- **Journal and site.** Record `buy-burn-child` events with the SOL spent and coin burned. The site relabels the two
  parent buckets as "coin buyback budget", shows the claim window with its closing time, and shows the burned
  amounts from KIDSPAR1 offsets 224 and 232 after the burn.

## Keeper, API and site for build 6 (patched 24 September 2026, not deployed)

The "What the keeper needs" list above is now written. Everything is keyed on the live build's feature list (the manifest's
`features`, recorded by the supervisor from `deployment/MAINNET-IDENTITIES.json` and refreshed live by `atomicContext` after
an upgrade), never on a date or an env setting. Against build 5 the keeper, the API and the site behave exactly as today.

- **Keeper** (`localnet/active-fee-keeper.mjs`, `localnet/atomic-fees.mjs`, `localnet/atomic-claims.mjs`). With
  `child-buyback`: `feePlan` emits one `buy-burn-child` for allocated A+B minus spent A+B (a `buy-burn-child-waiting` event
  below the buyback minimum) and never a parent `buy-burn`; the tick quotes the slice (at most 0.5 SOL, at most the pending
  budget) with `boundedQuote` on the campaign pool, applies the reserve-based impact cap (halving down to 0.05 SOL, no Pyth),
  passes `min_out` at the program floor or stricter, sends tag 27 through the operator sender with the same journaling;
  a refusal is released under the `child` bucket. With `parent-claim-expiry`: from `launch_time + 2,592,000` the keeper
  sends tag 11 once (KIDSPAR1 offset 240 is the guard), journaled as `parents-expired-burn` with both burned amounts; a
  program refusal backs off an hour, a transport error resumes. Signer policy: tags 11 and 27 added to `KEEPER_TAGS`
  (tag 11 has no signer; the operator only pays the fee); `DEFAULT_LIMITS` unchanged.
- **API.** `parentStats`, `postlaunchClaims` and the post-launch state expose `expiresAtUnix` (null on build 5), the window
  flags, `burnedRaw` per parent from offsets 224/232 and `burnedAtUnix`; the fee block adds `childBuybackPending` (chain),
  `childBuybackSpent` and `childBoughtAndBurned` (the keeper's running totals; the chain counters mix the parent purchases
  made before the upgrade and the tag 26 burns, so they cannot give these). `programFeatures` and `parentClaimWindow` are
  served. Claim prepare for a parent refuses after the window with a plain sentence.
- **Site.** Claim panel: "Claim by <UTC>" from the served window, then "Parent claims closed on … · unclaimed rewards
  burned" with the amount once tag 11 has run. Coin page: one "Shartcoin buyback and burn" tile and token-details row on
  build 6 (earlier parent burns kept on a small line), the parent tiles on build 5. Docs: `docsFor(programFeatures)` swaps
  the fee-routing, buyback and claim-window copy. Activity feed: tags 11 and 27 decoded and labelled.
- **Still open.** `verify-atomic-fees.mjs` and `qualify-active-lifecycle.mjs` (the localnet lifecycle rehearsal) still
  exercise tags 24 and 25 and need a tag 27 path before they can run against build 6.

## Rollout order (mirrors build 5)

1. **Park stays on.** Leave `KIDS_BUYBACK_MIN_LAMPORTS` at its parked value on `kids-api`. The keeper then logs
   `buyback-waiting-for-budget` for the whole bucket and sends no buyback, on either build.
2. **CI build.** Push the program source so `sbf-build.yml` prints the build 6 hash on both runners. Record `sha256` and
   `binarySize` under version 6 in `deployment/MAINNET-IDENTITIES.json` (both are `null` now) and commit.
   Do not set `KIDS_PROGRAM_SHA256` on `kids-api`: the supervisor takes an env build with an empty feature list, which
   would make the keeper plan build 5 operations against build 6.
3. **Owner upgrades the program** with the governance key, as for builds 2, 4 and 5 (`KIDS_PROGRAM_SHA256` is only the
   deploy tool's check of the local binary; the binary is the CI artifact):

   ```sh
   cd ~/kids && git pull
   KIDS_NETWORK=mainnet KIDS_HELIUS_RPC_URL="$KIDS_HELIUS_RPC_URL" KIDS_PROGRAM_SHA256=<ci sha256 of build 6> \
     node babies-launchpad/localnet/deploy-program-network.mjs deployment-artifacts/kids_atomic_launch.so --check-only
   KIDS_NETWORK=mainnet KIDS_HELIUS_RPC_URL="$KIDS_HELIUS_RPC_URL" KIDS_PROGRAM_SHA256=<ci sha256 of build 6> \
     node babies-launchpad/localnet/deploy-program-network.mjs deployment-artifacts/kids_atomic_launch.so
   solana program show BLiaZWNQoPm4mG4cXNm4sXifFqs1Xmxx12qD9T4Y5NeN --url "$KIDS_HELIUS_RPC_URL" --output json
   ```

   The build is smaller than build 5, so no `solana program extend` is expected. A build 5 keeper against build 6 fails every
   parent buyback with `InvalidInstructionData` until step 4; with the park on, none is attempted.
4. **Manifest and features.** The running service matches the new bytes against version 6 of the identities file at its next
   qualification (event `program-upgraded-live` with the build 6 features). Then push `main` with this patch so `kids-api`
   rebuilds and `kids.fun` redeploys; at boot the supervisor logs `program-verified` with the build 6 hash and writes the
   manifest with `parent-claim-expiry` and `child-buyback`. Check:

   ```sh
   curl -s https://kids.fun/api/account/postlaunch | jq '{programFeatures, parentClaimWindow, fees: .fees | {childBuybackPending, childBuybackSpent, childBoughtAndBurned}}'
   ```

   Expected: both features listed, `parentClaimWindow.expiresAtUnix` = `1790213669` (launch time, window zero), `childBuybackPending` = the combined
   pending budget, spent and burned `"0"`.
5. **Unpark.** Delete `KIDS_BUYBACK_MIN_LAMPORTS` on `kids-api` (the default minimum of 0.005 SOL applies) and redeploy.
   The keeper's next tick plans `buy-burn-child`; the first confirmed slice appears as `buy-burn-child` in `feeEvents` and
   in the coin page's activity feed with the SOL out and the Shartcoin burned, and the "Shartcoin buyback and burn" tile
   starts counting.
6. **Right after the upgrade** the keeper sends tag 11 on its first tick (the window is already closed) (event `parents-expired-burn`
   in `feeEvents` with both amounts), or the owner sends it by hand; the claim panel then shows the closed state with the
   burned amount.
