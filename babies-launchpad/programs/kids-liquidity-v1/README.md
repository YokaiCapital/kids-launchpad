# Direct liquidity v1 — implementation kernel

**Not deployed, not a Solana entrypoint, and not ready to hold funds.** This crate implements the
arithmetic, policy encoding and state transitions for the new Direct feature. It deliberately
builds as a Rust library only. There are no CPI, signing, transfer, initialization or deployment
instructions. Unit tests do not establish on-chain custody safety.

Existing parent/Family launches and existing Standard launches are separate and unchanged.
This crate does not depend on or modify their programs. A new controller and new Direct issuer
must eventually use new program IDs and program-owned accounts.

## Implemented

- Exactly 50% of initially received LP goes to temporary custody; the other half, including any
  odd raw-unit remainder, is permanent. This splits LP units, not total token supply.
- One cycle redeems floor(remaining temporary LP × 300 / 10,000). It cannot consume donated LP
  or use 3% of the original budget on every run.
- Explicit first-delay parameter (at least one day), chain-time-compatible rolling daily
  schedule, successful-cycle sequence, and no catch-up purchases after downtime.
- Explicit ending rule: until raw LP dust, or after a chosen count of successful cycles. There
  is no implicit 90-day expiration. After 90 successful cycles, for example, remaining LP is
  eligible for locking immediately; there is no 91st purchase. A cycle limit is not a wall-clock
  sunset when price guards or outages skip execution.
- A lock-remainder transition requiring equal temporary debit and canonical-lock credit,
  without reducing LP mint supply. Donations remain outside the tracked budget.
- Cycle-effect validation: LP custody, actual LP mint supply and AMM internal LP supply each
  decrease by the authorized slice; child mint supply decreases by both nonzero burns;
  withdrawn SOL equals swap input; working custody balances return to their original amounts.
- A canonical 32-byte policy codec and 128-byte draft kernel-state codec. Malformed versions,
  unsupported split/rate, hidden padding, impossible custody trajectories, counter overflow,
  replay and rounding-to-zero cycles are rejected. Decoder work is bounded independently of
  a submitted cycle count.
- An offline classic-SPL/WSOL constant-product estimator. Its usable reserves exclude accrued
  protocol/fund fees. It is NOT a reference price or exact deployed-Raydium parity proof.

**Owner decision, 25 September 2026: preserve treasury/dev fee entitlements from both LP
halves.** `RecycleWithPrincipal` remains historical research only and fails the release
precondition. `PreserveTreasuryDev` is recorded but fails with `FeeAccountingNotImplemented`:
its harvesting and principal accounting are not implemented. It cannot silently encode as the
old fee policy or use the old simulator. The draft codec/model is not a production policy.

The temporary position needs verified fee harvesting/accounting before recycling principal.
Preserve the existing token-side fee burn policy too; do not reinterpret this choice as paying
token-side fees to treasury/dev. The remainder budget and fee receipts must not double-count
fees, LP units spent harvesting, donations or fees generated by the recycling swap. The amount
of earnings still depends on actual remaining liquidity and trading volume; this choice does
not guarantee the same future revenue as retaining all original LP forever.

## Required next layer

1. Implement the selected fee-preservation accounting and settle the stopping policy. Keep the existing commitments, soft/hard caps,
   proportional allocations, refunds and dev vesting in the new Direct issuer; no parent
   fields, allocations or buybacks in Direct. Preserve all old routes.
2. Define a new sealed campaign layout and controller identity envelope binding the genesis,
   issuer program, controller program, campaign, same pool, AMM/config, child/WSOL/LP mints,
   permanent-lock evidence, fee rights and complete price/execution policy. Hash all terms
   before commitments. This draft kernel codec alone is NOT that envelope.
3. Authenticate the issuer-owned campaign and its PDA signer. Reject old campaigns/programs.
   At pool creation atomically lock half of measured LP and transfer the other half into the
   new controller's exclusive PDA custody. No external delegate or discretionary recipient.
4. Implement fixed Raydium withdraw → SPL BurnChecked → same-pool WSOL-to-child swap →
   SPL BurnChecked CPIs in one controller instruction. Read all `Effects` from authenticated
   chain accounts before/after the CPIs; never accept effects, pool balances, timestamps or
   execution authorization from a client. Verify owner, PDA, mint, token program, delegate,
   close/freeze/mint authority, canonical program and account aliases. Keep permanent LP and
   user reserves out of the writable execution account set.
5. Add a reviewed, authenticated price reference with freshness/history/deviation/impact
   guards. Current-pool-derived minOut alone is not protection against a prior manipulation.
   The estimator in this crate must not authorize execution. Decide minimum economic batch,
   operational funding and cost limits. Fees/rent/tips must not consume withdrawn SOL.
6. Add permissionless canonical remainder locking and a separate constrained donation-lock
   path; do not make donated LP expand the cycle budget or fake a burn receipt.
7. Rehearse against pinned Raydium binaries and a real validator, checking token supply and
   all reserves with actual program CPIs, failures, replay, malicious accounts, outside LP,
   donations, fee collection, manipulated prices and outage recovery. Measure SBF stack,
   compute and transaction size. Host effects tests cannot replace this.
8. Integrate version-specific registry/SDK/signing checks, durable keeper intents/reconciliation,
   public evidence and the Direct UI; independent review, program authority verification and
   explicit activation follow. Never market the temporary half as permanently locked or a
   guaranteed SOL floor. Publish actual withdrawal/burn receipts.

The build intentionally has no default production parameters, no program keypair, no deployment
script and no entrypoint, preventing this incomplete integration from accepting money.

## Verification

```sh
cargo test --manifest-path programs/kids-liquidity-v1/Cargo.toml --offline
node --test localnet/recycling/direct-policy.test.mjs
```

`localnet/recycling/direct-policy.mjs` matches the draft policy encoding and provides offline
simulations. The older `model.mjs` retains its historical 90/10 and 95/5 research envelope;
its tests are not substituted for the new Direct tests.
